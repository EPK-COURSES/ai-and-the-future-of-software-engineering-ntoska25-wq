While researching this topic, I learned that AI is changing software engineering much faster than I expected. Before doing this research, I thought AI mainly helped programmers write code faster. However, I discovered that the most important skills in the future may not be coding itself, but understanding problems, making decisions, and evaluating AI-generated solutions.
The most surprising thing for me was how important critical thinking remains. Even if AI can generate large amounts of code, developers still need to verify that the code is correct and safe. This showed me that software engineers cannot rely completely on AI tools.
One concern I have is that students may become too dependent on AI. If developers stop practicing programming fundamentals, they may struggle when AI makes mistakes or when they face complex problems that require deeper understanding.
At the same time, I see many opportunities. AI can reduce repetitive work and allow engineers to focus on more creative and important tasks. It can also help developers learn new technologies faster and become more productive.
As a first-year Software Engineering student, this research helped me understand that learning programming fundamentals is still very important. In the future, I plan to continue improving my coding skills while also learning how to use AI tools effectively. I believe the best software engineers will be those who combine strong technical knowledge with the ability to work alongside AI responsibly.
